My world is Blue

Poem by Kathryn Napoles

I onced lived a life that was blue, what was new? Same lonely blue living room, one lone blue mug of bitter cup of coffee on my blue table, and a wide garden with one lone blue flower, stands alone facing the sun.That was until I found her, a girl who loved blue. When she came into my life, everything changed. Somehow the same blue living room was filled with laughter, beside my cup of bitter coffee was her own mug full of ice cold matcha. But most of all, the garden where the lone blue flower stood, was now full of various (still blue) flowers. And now, I live a blue life with my lovely wife.
